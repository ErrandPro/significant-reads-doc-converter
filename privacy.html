3. Information We Collect or Process

The Service is designed to work without an account and without payment details. Running a website still involves some information, described below.

You may upload Word documents, PDF files, JPG images, and PNG images for conversion or processing. These files may contain personal data, copyrighted material, confidential information, or information about other people. We process their contents only to perform the operation you request.

We process files temporarily, return the result to you, and do not keep your files or results as a permanent document-storage service. We do not sell the contents of your files, use them for advertising, or use them for unrelated purposes.

Because files are processed on our hosting infrastructure, that provider's systems handle them as part of delivering the Service. This handling is limited to what is needed to provide, secure, and maintain the Service.

3.2 Information entered into conversion forms

For EPUB output, you can enter details such as:

We use these entries to generate the output you request and do not keep them as a profile or content record afterwards. Associated technical information may still appear briefly in server logs or diagnostic records. Please do not enter information that isn't needed for your conversion.

6. How Uploaded Files Are Handled

Uploaded files and form entries are used to perform the operation you requested and for the technically necessary processing involved in delivering it. We do not inspect files for unrelated commercial purposes or use their contents to build advertising profiles.

6.2 Temporary processing and deletion

The Service is designed for temporary processing, not permanent storage. A converted result is prepared for a single download: once you have downloaded it, it is deleted from our processing systems. Results that are never downloaded are removed automatically after a short period.

Short-lived technical copies may exist in temporary processing queues and in backups or logs maintained by our hosting provider while a job runs. These are not used as a collection of user documents.

6.3 No file recovery service

We do not keep backups of uploaded files or converted results. Please download your result and keep your own copies.

6.4 Sensitive documents

We recommend that you avoid uploading documents containing highly sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account credentials, medical records, confidential business information, or legally privileged material unless necessary and you have assessed the risks.
